My Story

Hey there! I'm Kaawya Chandrasekara, an 18-year-old developer and entrepreneur from Kalutara, Sri Lanka. My journey started back in 2021 with digital marketing and social media management.

Since then, I've launched multiple businesses including DMK Store, acquired companies like Ru Online Stores, founded DMC Group, and currently running Vezlo Web Development. I'm also the founder of Global Law - a legal information platform making law accessible to everyone.
