My Story

Hey there! I'm Kaawya, an ambitious young developer and entrepreneur from the beautiful coastal city of Kalutara, Sri Lanka. My journey into the digital world began back in 2021, starting with digital marketing and social media management.

Since then, I've launched multiple successful businesses including DMK Store, acquired companies like Ru Online Stores, and founded DMC Group as my parent company umbrella. I'm currently diving deep into full-stack web development, mastering technologies like Next.js, Supabase, React, and more.

"I believe in building products that solve real problems and create meaningful impact in people's lives."

When I'm not coding or running businesses, you'll find me exploring cutting-edge AI/ML technologies, learning about cybersecurity, or connecting with like-minded individuals who share my passion for innovation! 🚀
